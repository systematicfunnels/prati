# PRD

## Product Understanding
- Platform type: Enterprise SaaS platform for operational intelligence.
- Primary goal: Present PRATI as a premium, visual-first platform website that explains the core in seconds.
- Success metric: A buyer should understand problem, platform logic, architecture, industry fit, and pilot CTA without reading dense blocks of text.

## Target Users
- Operations leaders
- Facility and infrastructure managers
- Industrial innovation teams
- Drone and remote operations teams

## Value Proposition
- One shared operational truth across telemetry, assets, alerts, and AI-ready context.
- Configurable rollout model instead of custom engineering for every environment.
- Clear path from live monitoring to AI inference and copilots.

## Scope
- Redesign homepage information density.
- Reuse supplied illustration assets as primary storytelling devices.
- Preserve enterprise tone and strong CTA for pilot engagement.

## Non-Goals
- Deep product documentation on the homepage
- Full marketing automation or CRM integration
- Expanded app-level feature development beyond landing experience
